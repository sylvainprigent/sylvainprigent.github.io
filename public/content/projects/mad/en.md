# Melasma Automatic Detector — AI-based clinical imaging analytics tool (Dermatology / Pharma R&D)

## Context

Clinical evaluation of dermatological treatments traditionally relies on subjective visual assessments by dermatologists, leading to variability in scoring and long validation cycles.

The objective of this research project conducted in collaboration with Galderma was to investigate the use of multi- and hyperspectral imaging combined with machine learning to provide objective, quantitative measurements of treatment effectiveness in clinical studies.

The project aimed to design an automated analytics tool capable of tracking patient evolution over time and generating reproducible severity scores to accelerate decision-making in treatment development.
