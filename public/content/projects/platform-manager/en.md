# Platform-Manager — Operations digitalization platform for technical facilities (Research / Laboratory environments)

## Context

Technical research facilities rely on fragmented tools and manual processes to manage resource booking, project tracking, inventory, billing and quality documentation, creating operational inefficiencies and limiting traceability required for certified environments.

The objective of Platform-Manager was to design and deploy a modular web platform centralizing operational workflows in order to improve administrative efficiency, support ISO9001 quality processes, and enable scalable deployment across multiple laboratory facilities.

## Role — Solution Architect / Full-Stack Platform Engineer

* Designed and developed a modular operations management platform supporting multiple technical facilities
* Translated laboratory operational constraints into structured digital workflows and data models
* Led technical roadmap definition and deployment strategy
* Delivered end-to-end solution lifecycle from architecture to user adoption

## Key Challenges

* Digitalizing heterogeneous administrative workflows across facilities
* Ensuring data consistency and traceability aligned with quality certification requirements
* Supporting multi-site deployment and configuration flexibility
* Automating billing and quotation processes linked to project activities
* Designing a system usable by non-technical operational staff

## Contributions & Achievements

* Designed centralized SQL data models supporting inventory management, project lifecycle tracking and laboratory-specific datasets (e.g. antibody management)
* Developed modular web application features for booking, invoicing, quotation generation and operational reporting
* Implemented automation workflows significantly reducing manual administrative effort
* Delivered containerized deployment approach (Docker) enabling reproducible installations across ~30 facilities
* Structured documentation, training sessions and product demonstrations to support large-scale user adoption
* Contributed to aligning platform capabilities with ISO9001 process requirements and audit traceability needs

## Technologies & Practices

**Delivery**

* Agile / Scrum

## Outcomes

* Deployment of Platform-Manager across ~30 technical facilities
* Reduction of administrative workload by a factor of 5 for operational teams
* Improved process standardization and audit readiness in certified environments
* Increased resource utilization visibility and financial tracking reliability
