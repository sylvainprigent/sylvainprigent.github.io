# Eve Analytics — Large-scale microscopy data platform (Pharma / Pre-clinical R&D) 

## Context

Pharma research teams generate massive volumes of time-resolved 3D microscopy data (>1TB per experiment) using Nanolive microscopes. Existing workflows were manual, fragmented, and unable to scale, creating bottlenecks in data exploitation, reproducibility and decision-making speed. 

The objective was to design and industrialize Eve Analytics, a high-performance analytics platform enabling automated processing and visualization of complex microscopy datasets while meeting strict confidentiality, on-prem deployment and scientific validation constraints. 

## Role — Data Solution Architect 

* Led end-to-end architecture design of a modular analytics platform for scientific imaging workflows 
* Translated pre-clinical research needs into scalable data & compute architecture 
* Acted as interface between R&D scientists, product management and engineering teams 
* Defined technical strategy, performance targets and validation approach 

## Key Challenges 

* Processing 2D / 3D / 3D+t imaging datasets at scale with near real-time constraints 
* Guaranteeing processing time lower than acquisition time
* Supporting fully isolated on-prem deployments for pharma clients 
* Ensuring scientific reproducibility and validation traceability 
* Adapting architecture to heterogeneous customer IT environments 

## Contributions & Achievements 

* Designed a high-performance data processing pipeline combining Python / C++ computation modules and optimized storage strategies 
* Delivered proofs of concept validating architecture scalability on >1TB datasets 
* Defined data models and database strategy enabling structured experiment tracking and result reproducibility 
* Proposed performance optimization strategies (parallelization, memory management, algorithm refactoring) significantly reducing processing latency 
* Facilitated design workshops with research teams to align scientific workflows with product capabilities 
* Built user story mapping and technical roadmap supporting the Eve 2.0 product vision 
* Established testing & validation framework on reference pre-clinical datasets to support scientific credibility 
* Contributed to UX design decisions improving usability for non-technical researchers 
* Delivered technical enablement for sales and customer teams to support solution adoption
